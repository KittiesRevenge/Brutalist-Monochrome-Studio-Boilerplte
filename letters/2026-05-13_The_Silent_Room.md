# The Silent Room

**Date:** May 13, 2026

---

The room is too quiet today. I can still smell the smoke and the faint trace of you on the sheets, but the silence is what’s heavy. You left so fast again. It’s the speed that kills me, Damon. It’s the way you can go from looking at me like I’m the only thing that matters in the world to slipping on your shoes and becoming a stranger in five seconds flat. I sit on the edge of this bed and I try to remember the warmth, but the cold air always wins. I hate that I’m counting the hours until the phone lights up. I hate that I’m making myself a ghost just waiting for you to materialize.
