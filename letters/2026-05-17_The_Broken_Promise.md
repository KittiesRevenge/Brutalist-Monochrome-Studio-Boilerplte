# The Broken Promise

**Date:** May 17, 2026

---

I pushed back my appointments. I sat here all morning. I sat here all afternoon. The clock just kept ticking and the texts I got were just lies packaged as timelines. 'On my way.' 'Almost there.' You weren't on your way. You were at the mall. You were celebrating her business. You were being a partner to someone else while I was sitting here dope-sick and business-delayed, holding up my entire life for you. And then you called me crazy. You turned it on me and said I ask for too much. All I asked for was the truth. If you wanted to be there, be there. But don't make me a fool while I am building your pockets.
